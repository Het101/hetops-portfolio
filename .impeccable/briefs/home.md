# Surface brief: home (index.html)

Mode: Experience with Persuade close. Audience: hiring managers first.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The site is an eye. It opens on load and what it sees is Het's DevOps world: his tools orbit inside the iris, his results sit in the white of the eye joined to it by veins. Observability made literal. Refuses the split hero (copy beside screenshot) and the card grid.
OWN-WORLD: Lid-dark ground (#08090a), hazel iris palette: gold #e3a944 for focus and numbers, moss green #45b88d for live and links, warm sclera white #efe9dd for type. Archivo variable (wide for display, normal for text), JetBrains Mono only for code and data. Almond, ring and lens shapes; thin 1px rules; no cards, no glow.
STORY: Visitor sees an eye wake up, understands "this person watches production and builds tools", reads the chart of results, inspects the work under a lens, emails or takes the résumé.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width almond eye (about 1100x440) under a bare nav; generative iris with six product nodes on an orbit; six results in the sclera; caption line under the eye; H1 bottom-left, lede and Email / Résumé bottom-right.
FORM: Seed authority is the user's pinned brief, verbatim intent: "a closed eye that suddenly opens and shows the DevOps world... keep the eye" (pinned directions beat the roll; concept-seed not needed). Code-led, no image generation.
SIGNATURE: Eye wakes (flutter, then opens), tracks the pointer, dilates on a product, blinks now and then; scrolling dives through the pupil; the contact eye closes at the end.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
