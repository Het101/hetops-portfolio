# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want hetops-portfolio to be a project people are glad they contributed to. Everyone taking part — maintainers, contributors, people pointing out a broken link — deserves a harassment-free experience, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Things that make this project work:

- Assuming the person on the other end is competent and short on time
- Giving and taking feedback on the code rather than the person
- Saying "I was wrong" early and without ceremony
- Remembering that someone filing a bug report took time out of their day to do it

Things that will not be tolerated:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission
- Pressuring anyone to share credentials or private data in an issue or PR

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer by email at patel.x.het@gmail.com, or through [GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/Het101/hetops-portfolio/security/advisories/new). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers will remove, edit, or reject contributions that do not align with this code of conduct, and will explain moderation decisions when asked. Maintainers who do not follow it in good faith may face consequences decided by the project's other maintainers.

### Scale of response

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was wrong and why.
2. **Warning** — consequences stated for continued behaviour, with a period of no interaction with those involved.
3. **Temporary ban** — from any interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban.**
